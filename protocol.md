# Completed supplementary experiment protocol

The dated original proposal is preserved in `protocol_original_20261112.md`. This document describes the executed, completed scope; original six-block proposals are not the final baseline sample size.

The authorized shortened design retains all 216 requests on D1 and all 216 on D2, seven reading paths, two paired query blocks per dataset, one planned warm-up and three formal calls per request/path. The completed counts are 18,144 formal calls and 6,048 planned warm-ups. Chat-controlled pauses and resumed process segments contribute 5,453 additional recovery warm-up calls, excluded from medians. There are 204 fresh layout builds over 34 dataset/layout combinations with six repetitions each.

The final source manifest combines the original completed D1 block with three completed request-group-resumable blocks. Seven paths complete a request group before a checkpoint commits it. Resuming starts a new process segment, warms remaining requests separately and continues the fixed schedule. Segment IDs are provenance, not independent statistical units. Four calls in one incomplete recovery group are preserved as excluded evidence. Earlier timeout and cleanup records are retained; they are not substituted for successful worker output. Session-level authorization, confirmed pause intervals, process identities and extended budgets are checked against every included call.

The original initial 01:00–08:00 window was modified by explicit, recorded author instructions. The manifest and archived ledgers contain actual policies for each session rather than retroactively applying one universal window. No new formal measurement is necessary for this release.

Median-call selection, stage attribution, two-block paired ratios, six-build arithmetic preparation costs, 253-mixture amortization and independent recomputation are specified in the supplement and executable replay. The original six-block layout results and frozen D2 predictions are unchanged. Threshold sensitivities are post hoc analyses; none selects new D2 parameters.
